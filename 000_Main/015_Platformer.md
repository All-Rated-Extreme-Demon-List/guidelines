## Platformer {:platformer}
On AREDL, platformer submissions follow the [Pemonlist guidelines](https://pemonlist.com/rules). We highly recommend you read them in full, but these are the main things you need to know.
- Your completion must show a full run with the completion of each checkpoint and the endscreen.
- Your completion video and raw footage must have all clicks fully audible throughout the entire completion.
- You must record and upload a video of your completion to YouTube or another video sharing platform.
- Completion videos may not be deleted after they are accepted.
- Your record may not use any [disallowed mods](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13zirLi-ociDBJ8QyRZs803_WAm68HiSHtnyoAnNPncw/edit?gid=0#gid=0). **For platformers, this includes the Click Between Frames (CBF) mod and Platformer Saves (Geode) mod being disallowed.**
- If your video was uploaded after July 2nd, 2023, your record must have all cheat indicators visible on the level's end screen.
- Your record must show the stats on the endscreen (attempts, orbs, etc.). *If the completion was a rebeat, you must specify so in the notes.*
- During your completion, you may not use any skips that make any section of the level significantly easier.
