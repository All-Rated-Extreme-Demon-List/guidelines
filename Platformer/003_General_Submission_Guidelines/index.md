# General Submission Guidelines {:general-submission-guidelines}

* These rules apply to Pemonlist, TPL, and AREDL platformer submissions. Some rules apply to certain lists differently. Any differences between the lists are explicitly stated in these rules.  
* **The in-game Show Time feature is required and must be enabled, even on vanilla Geometry Dash.** Please ensure this setting (or an acceptable alternative) is turned on during your entire completion.  
* Ensure the correct level ID and/or correct level is chosen on the pop-up menu (this is specifically the case with levels that have the identical or similar names).  
* The “Completion Link” / “Link” field refers to the video you would like to be displayed on the website.  
* The "Raw Footage" field refers to unedited recording of the entire completion. See the [Raw Footage section](#raw-footage) for more info on raw footage.  
* Some levels have special requirements for submissions. See the [Special Submission Requirements document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RGs1yS5sdH7upDNL726SIk-XY9qGvVHr6gUP4ZHupN4/edit?usp=sharing) for a complete list of all levels with special requirements. See the [Special Submission Requirements section](#special-submission-requirements) of these rules for more info.  
* If you have submitted a record and realized you have made a mistake (e.g., inputted the wrong video link or wrong level), contact a member of the records team or in the support channel in the Discord server.  
* Rules do not apply retroactively. If there is a rule change, and your completion was provably done before the rule change was put in effect, it should not be rejected for breaking that rule. Contact us if a record is wrongfully rejected due to a new rule that took effect after your completion.
