## Audio Requirements

* The footage must include microphone audio with audible clicks/taps.  
* The mic audio (i.e., clicks/taps) must be on its own audio track, separate from the in-game audio. There must be at least one audio track where the mic audio is the only audio audible on that track.  
* If you do not want to record multiple audio tracks, recording the mic audio and not recording the in-game audio is acceptable.  
* **If you upload your raw footage to YouTube, you cannot include in-game audio in your raw footage, as YouTube only saves one audio track.**  
* If you do not know how to record multiple audio tracks on PC, here are some guides explaining how to configure this with some popular recording software:  
  * [OBS Studio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YNlRUVk3oE8)  
  * [Medal.tv](https://support.medal.tv/support/solutions/articles/48001220541-multi-track-recording)  
  * [GeForce Experience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IP-3dJw0uMY)  
* On mobile, there is not any well-known accessible way to record multiple tracks in one recording. One workaround is to record in-game audio (and optionally mic audio) with your desired screen recording software, and simultaneously run your phone’s built-in Voice Recorder app to record your mic audio.  
* If you have not recorded raw footage before, it is recommended that you make a test recording to ensure your footage/audio is recording properly to follow our guidelines.
