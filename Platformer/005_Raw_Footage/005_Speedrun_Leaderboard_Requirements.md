## Speedrun leaderboard requirements

* **If you would like your time to be listed on a level page’s speedrun leaderboard, you must meet the following additional requirements:**  
  * You must beat the level in **one single session**, meaning you cannot exit the level and later resume at the last completed checkpoint (such as through the in-game Load Save feature).  
  * You must record the entire completion in **one single uncut, unedited recording**. This means you cannot send raw footage where one completion is done across multiple recordings/videos.  
* **If your submission does not meet this specific requirement, you can still get your submission accepted, you can still get list points, and your time will still be visible to others** — your record will simply be listed separate from the speedrun leaderboard on the level page.
