# Raw Footage {:raw-footage}

* “Raw footage” is defined as footage that is not edited using a video editing tool in any way.  
* You must include an unedited recording of your completion showing the full run with the completion of each checkpoint and the endscreen.  
  * A full recording is ideal, **but at least 5 minutes for each checkpoint is required** (or the full recording from starting attempts at the checkpoint).  
  * Different recordings cannot be spliced into one video as your raw footage. If you would like, you can upload the videos in one YouTube playlist, or include the links to all raw footage in the description of the first video.  
* You must have the Show Time feature enabled for the full duration of your recording. Some mods/mod features are an acceptable alternative to this, such as Mega Hack’s Level Time feature. See our mods spreadsheet for more info (which is listed in the [Modding section](#modding) below).  
* Uploading to sites such as Google Drive / MEGA / Gofile is ideal so the records team can download the video.  
* Sites such as YouTube are acceptable, but you must keep the file(s) of the video for at least 2 weeks after your record gets accepted, in case the records team requests it.
