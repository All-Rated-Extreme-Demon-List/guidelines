# Quality {:quality}

* The following are reasons a record could be unverifiable due to issues with the footage:  
  * Extremely low resolution  
  * Severe framerate drops  
  * Covered gameplay  
  * Inaudible clicks  
  * If you are recording with an external camera: low visibility, bad camera/footage quality, or a bad camera angle  
* If you are unsure if your footage is of acceptable quality, contact a member of the records team or in the support channel in the Discord server.  
* While input display is NOT a replacement for clicks, using it could help verify the legitimacy of your record. Input display is not required for most records (see the [Special Submission Requirements section](#special-submission-requirements) for info on when it is required).  
* Usage of any "Click Sounds" or "Click Bot" mod/feature (for example, to replace or overlay real clicks) is prohibited and will deem your record illegitimate.
