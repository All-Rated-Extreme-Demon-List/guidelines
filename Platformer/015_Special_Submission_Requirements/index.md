# Special Submission Requirements {:special-submission-requirements}

* This section describes types of levels that have additional submission requirements. Potential additional requirements for a level may include:  
  * Input display  
  * Handcam  
  * Full footage of entire run, including failed attempts  
* **The rules in this section only apply to specific levels, and do not apply to most levels.**  
* We have listed all levels that currently have additional requirements in this [Special Submission Requirements document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RGs1yS5sdH7upDNL726SIk-XY9qGvVHr6gUP4ZHupN4/edit?usp=sharing). **You are responsible for reviewing this document so that you are aware of any levels with special requirements and/or understand the rules behind the special requirements.**  
* The rules behind these special submission requirements are significantly more complex compared to all other rules; since they are difficult to understand and only apply to a handful of levels, the rules are laid out in greater detail on the Special Submission Requirements document, instead of here.  
* **If you are creating or verifying a level that you would like to be listed on TPL** and you do not understand the rules on the “Rules” tab of the Special Submission Requirements document, consider contacting a staff member on the TPL team to ensure the verification submission for your level does not break any special submission requirements.
