# Pause Abuse {:pause-abuse}

* Using the pause menu to assist in making inputs is not allowed. Examples of pause abuse include but are not limited to the following:  
  * Pause buffering – an exploit involving rapid pausing and unpausing in order to effectively play in slow motion  
  * Pause canceling – an alternate method of releasing inputs by pausing while holding and releasing before unpausing, usually used to make short presses more consistent  
  * Pausing strategically to assist with reaction time  
* Not all uses of the pause menu are banned; normal use of pausing will not be suspected as pause abuse. Examples of acceptable pausing include but are not limited to the following:  
  * Pausing to take a break  
  * Pausing to think before continuing a timed section in a way that does not significantly improve reaction times  
  * Pausing that gives no advantage
