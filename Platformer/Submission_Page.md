### We **highly** recommend you [read the full guidelines here](/guidelines/?list=platformer), but these are the main things you need to know.

* Your raw footage must have all clicks fully audible throughout the entire completion.  
* You must have the in-game Show Time setting enabled, even in vanilla Geometry Dash. Any acceptable modded alternatives are explicitly stated in the full ruleset.  
* A full recording is ideal, but at least 5 minutes for each checkpoint is required (or the full recording from starting attempts at the checkpoint).  
* Your record must have all cheat indicators visible on the level’s endscreen.  
* You must have isolated clicks for your raw footage.  
* If you would like to be listed on the speedrun leaderboard, you additionally need to beat the level in one session and record the entire completion in one single uncut, unedited recording for your raw footage.  
* Your record may not use any disallowed mods.  
* Pause abuse is not allowed.  
* If you are using a custom copy of a level, it must be approved by list staff.  
* During your completion, you may not use any skips that make the level significantly easier.  
* There are certain levels that have additional special submission requirements. This includes levels that require input display, handcam, and/or uncut raw footage. See [this document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RGs1yS5sdH7upDNL726SIk-XY9qGvVHr6gUP4ZHupN4/edit?usp=sharing) for the rules on this, as well as a list of levels for which these special submission requirements apply.  
* Globed 2P Completions are allowed, however, you need to include both POVs in the video or provide a link to watch the other POV, and the completion must be on an approved Globed 2P copy.
